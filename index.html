Welcome

My name is Diane Wang. I have a wonderful husband, Jeff, and 3 fantastic adult children, Daniel, Kiane and Kelli. I enjoy art and creating things, and have been exploring art late in life, following the road not taken in my younger years. I love creating fun themed environments for the kids at our church Vacation Bible School every summer. I also enjoy teaching and creating art projects and crafts for kids, and have spent many hours volunteering to teach art. I have recently become the Media Coordinator at my church, where I design graphics and work on the website. Faith in Jesus gives me hope and purpose in life. I hope to always be growing in life and faith.

When I have free time, I enjoy

  • Spending time with my family and friends
  • Traveling with my husband
  • Helping with ministries at my church

The GVBC website will hopefully be my favorite website when I am finished reorganzing it.
